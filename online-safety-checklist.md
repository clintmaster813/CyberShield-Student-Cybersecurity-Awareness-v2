# Online Safety Checklist

## Introduction

Online safety is being safe online by keeping your personal information, accounts and devices secure. This list contains some easy to understand steps that can help keep your cyber security safe.

## 1. Account Security

[ ] Use strong and unique passwords
[ ] Turn on Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
[ ] Don’t share your passwords with anyone.
[ ] Use a good password manager.
[ ] Regularly check recent logins to your account.

[ ] Change your passwords if you think they have been compromised.
## 2. Email/Messaging
[ ] Always verify messages.
[ ] Don’t click on any suspicious links.
[ ] Don’t download any unexpected attachments.
[ ] Double check email addresses and websites.
[ ] Never share any verification codes.
[ ] Report any suspicious phishing.
## 3. Device Safety
[ ] Keep your Operating Systems updated.
[ ] Use good security software.
[ ] Turn on your firewall.
[ ] Lock your device when not in use.

[ ] Download apps from a trusted source.
[ ] Regularly backup important files.
## 4. Social Media
[ ] Check your privacy settings regularly.
[ ] Don’t share any sensitive personal information.
[ ] Be careful who you connect with.
[ ] Be cautious around accounts you don’t know.
[ ] Don’t share live location information.
[ ] Report any suspicious accounts.
## 5. Public Wi-Fi
[ ] Don’t access any sensitive accounts on untrusted networks.
[ ] Check you are connecting to the right network.
[ ] Turn off automatic connections to unknown networks.
[ ] Only use sites with HTTPS.
[ ] Don’t connect to public Wi-Fi anymore.
## 6. If your account/device has been compromised
[ ] Secure your accounts.
[ ] Change any compromised passwords using a trusted device.
[ ] Turn on MFA.
[ ] Report any issues to the organisation.
[ ] Contact your bank if any financial information has been compromised.
[ ] Check your accounts/devices for any suspicious activity.

## Conclusion
Using these methods will greatly improve your personal cyber security. To keep yourself safe online you must be aware, act responsibly online and regularly check your security.

Think before you click! Keep your information safe and report any suspicious activity.
