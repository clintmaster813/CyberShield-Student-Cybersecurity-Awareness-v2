# Guide To Password Security

## 1 Introduction

Using passwords is one of the most common ways to keep digital accounts and personal information secure. This can however pose risks to users if the password they are using is weak or repeated. Good password management will help keep your information safe

## 2 Common Password Attacks

Brute Force Attack

Automated attacks which will try various combinations of passwords until the correct one is found.

Dictionary Attack

Trying to breaches your accounts using common words and phrases.

Credential Stuffing

Using details such as usernames and passwords that have been leaked in previous breaches.

Password Spraying

Trying a few common passwords on many accounts.

## 3 Elements Of A Strong Password

Your password should be:

Long and hard to guess

Contain a unique mix of characters

Avoid using any personal details such as birthdays and names

Never be repeated

Avoid common passwords.

Also having a long pass phrase using unrelated words will protect.

## 4 How To Keep Passwords Secure

Use unique passwords.

Use a reputable password manager.

Enable Multi-Factor Authenication.

Never let anyone know your password.

Avoid putting your password into any sus sites.

Change your password straight away if there is a chance your account has been compromised.

Ensure you have up to date recovery details.

## 5 What Is Multi-Factor Authentication?

Multi-Factor Authentication can help add a layer of security to your account by requiring extra verification.

This can include:

Authentication Apps

Security Keys

Biometrics.

It can help to avoid unauthorised access to your account even if your password has been compromised.

## 6 What To Do If Your Password Has Been Compromised

Change the password straight away via the official website.

Change the password on any other accounts where it is repeated.

Enable Multi-Factor authentication.

Check the accounts activity to see if there are any unknown logins.

If you are unable to access your account, contact the relevant provider.

## 7 Conclusion

Keeping your passwords secure is an important factor in keeping your digital identity safe. By using strong unique passwords along with Multi-Factor authentication and responsibilty when managing your accounts it will dramatically reduce the chances of unauthorised access.
